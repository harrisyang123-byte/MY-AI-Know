## 提示词

```
【整体场景】
Realistic architectural photography, top-down bird's-eye view of a Qing Dynasty tavern second floor,
Nanjing 1844, nighttime.

【方位定标 — 请严格按此理解以下所有"东/西/南/北"】
Compass orientation in this bird's-eye view:
- Top of image = NORTH = two large lattice windows, Qinhuai River night view beyond
- Bottom of image = SOUTH = passageway + camera position + stairway descending to first floor
- Right side of image = EAST = Li Hongzhang's seating side
- Left side of image = WEST = Zhang Shisan's seating side
- Southwest area of image = Gong Zizhen's table (freestanding, not against wall)

Warm amber lantern light from above, deep blue night light seeping in from the north windows.
Semi-open loft space overlooking the bustling first floor below, blurred figures and warm chatter
visible through the wooden railing gaps at the south edge.

【北墙 + 窗外景】
North wall: two large lattice windows pushed wide open, white paper on carved wooden frames,
Qinhuai River outside — dark water reflecting countless lantern lights from boats and riverside taverns.
A cool blue moonlight beam spills across the floorboards near the lattice, cutting a cool-toned path
across the warm amber wooden floor.

【中央方桌 + 椅子（重点修订）】
Center of the space: a square rosewood table (1.2m x 0.8m), carved edges, warm patina.
Already in the midst of drinking — a white porcelain wine flask half-empty, two small cups with
remaining wine catching the lamplight, a few cold dishes partially eaten, chopsticks resting
across a celery celadon porcelain rest. A small celadon oil lamp flickers amid the table setting,
casting a warm local glow.

【椅子 — 严格按东西轴向摆放】
Two rosewood armchairs (官帽椅) flank the square table on east and west sides ONLY:
- East-side chair (right side of image): facing WEST — Li Hongzhang's seat.
  Positioned at the east edge of the table, facing directly across to Zhang Shisan.
  A jade belt hangs from the chair back, suggesting the occupant has just stood up.
- West-side chair (left side of image): facing EAST — Zhang Shisan's seat.
  Positioned at the west edge of the table, angled approximately 10° toward the table center,
  as if the occupant leaned in during conversation.
The north and south edges of the table are deliberately open — no chairs, no obstructions.
Clear sightline from the south camera position, across the table surface, through to the
north windows and river view beyond.

【天花灯笼】
Above the table, a round silk lantern hangs from a dark ceiling beam, pale cream silk shade,
bronze fitting, short silk tassel. Its light diffuses softly across the half-finished table,
creating a warm oval pool of amber glow centered on the table surface.

【东南屏风】
Southeast corner of the dining area: a carved rosewood floor screen (落地屏风), approximately
1.8m wide, rice-white silk stretched over the frame, a faint ink landscape painting barely
visible on the silk. Furniture silhouettes on the other side appear soft and blurred through
the gauze. The screen separates the main dining area from the passageway and stairway beyond.

【走道 + 楼梯口】
Behind the screen (south of it): a narrow passageway leading to the stairway. Warm amber light
and muffled voices rising from the first floor below. Dark wooden railing at the south edge,
stairs descending.

【龚自珍桌 — 西南区独立摆放，不靠墙（重点修订）】
Southwest area of the room, approximately 2.5 meters diagonally from the center table:
a second rosewood dining table of the same type and dimensions, standing freely in the open space,
NOT pushed against any wall, NOT in a corner.
Simple setting — a single iron oil lamp casting a dim orange pool,
one white porcelain wine flask, one overturned cup beside it, no food dishes.
One wooden chair pushed back slightly from the table, angled outward — Gong Zizhen's seat, empty.
The table has clear space around it: at least 0.5m to the nearest wall on the west side,
open passage to the south toward the stairway.

【从机位到龚自珍 — 视线关系】
Camera tripod position is directly south of the center table, aligned on the north-south axis,
shooting north. From this position:
- Looking straight north: the center table fills the midground, north windows in the background
- Panning left (westward): the southwest area with Gong Zizhen's table is in direct line of sight
  — no screen obstruction, because the screen is on the east side (to the camera's right).
Between the camera and the center table: the translucent silk screen stands in the southeast quadrant,
partially veiling the view of the east passage but leaving the main table clearly visible.

【地面 + 墙面】
Dark wood floorboards with aged patina, warm reflection of lantern light on the polished surface.
Whitewashed plaster on partial walls, carved window frames. A small hanging scroll with ink
landscape painting on the east wall beside Li Hongzhang's seat.

【光线三层设计】
Naturalistic lighting with three distinct zones:
- Primary: Ceiling silk lantern directly above the center table, 2800K warm amber,
  creating a warm oval pool on the table surface and surrounding floor.
- Secondary: Gong Zizhen's iron oil lamp, 2600K warm orange, very dim and localized —
  this area is significantly darker than the center table area (approximately -2EV).
- Accent: Moonlight from the north windows, 6500K cool blue, spilling across the floorboards
  near the lattice, creating a cool blue crescent on the warm amber floor — the only cool-toned
  light in the space.

Key-to-fill ratio: 3:1 warm dominant at center table; 5:1 at Gong Zizhen's corner.
The contrast between warm interior and cool moonlight creates natural depth separation.

【画风标签】
hyperrealistic environment, cinematic Chinese architecture, volumetric lighting,
physically accurate lighting, in the visual style of Chang'an 30000 Miles background art,
3D Chinese animation aesthetic, Lights Animation Studio style
```

## 机位与龚自珍的视线关系（分镜参考）

从主机位（南侧朝北拍）能看到龚自珍的**三个方式**：

| 方式 | 运动 | 效果 |
|------|------|------|
| 1. 直接左摇（Pan left/West） | 从主桌 → 西南角 | 一瞬间看到龚自珍空桌，干净利落 |
| 2. 深景深同框 | 机位保持原位，广角容纳主桌+西南角 | 龚自珍在画面左下角作为背景细节 |
| 3. 切龚自珍POV | 西南角朝东北拍（S03-05b-v3已有） | 透过半透屏风看主桌两人剪影 |

**建议：** 方式1最实用——S03-06主桌双人对话拍完后，S03-06a或S03-07结束时，左摇到西南角，揭示龚自珍空椅+未喝完的酒——暗示他一直在暗处看着、已离去。
