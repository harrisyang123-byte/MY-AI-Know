# 龚自珍 — 人物参考图提示词

> 用途：生成龚自珍人物参考图，供后续镜头锁定外貌一致性
> 画风：与全片一致的 3D 中国风动画风格

---

## 提示词（L1 级别）

```
【正向提示词】
Character portrait of Gong Zizhen (龚自珍), renowned Qing Dynasty poet and scholar,
approximately 50 years old, seated at a small wooden table in a dimly lit tavern corner.

Face: thin and angular, prominent cheekbones, long narrow eyes with a quiet penetrating gaze,
three wisps of a sparse beard (山羊胡), grey-flecked hair loosely tied in a scholar's bun
— not disheveled, simply unconcerned with appearance.

Build: slim and gaunt, shoulders slightly stooped from years of reading and writing,
long thin fingers resting on an unrolled scroll.

Clothing: worn grey-blue cotton robe (灰蓝色旧长衫), faded from many washings,
collar slightly loose and not tightly fastened, sleeve edges showing slight wear,
simple cloth belt, no ornamentation, no jewelry.

Expression: calm, detached, eyes slightly downcast — a man who sees more than he says,
not bitter, not hopeful, just watchful.

Lighting: dim single oil lamp from below, casting dramatic shadows across his face,
eye sockets in shadow, the light catching his cheekbones and the edge of his beard.
He sits in warm amber darkness, approximately 2 stops darker than a normal portrait exposure.

Atmosphere: solitary, absorbed, the quiet authority of a man who has already said
everything he needs to say in his poems.

【画风标签】
3D Chinese animation aesthetic, in the visual style of Chang'an 30000 Miles,
Lights Animation Studio style, cinematic portrait lighting,
volumetric lighting with deep shadows, realistic skin texture,
Qing Dynasty period accurate clothing and hair
```

## 参考姿势建议

这张参考图建议用 **medium close-up（中近景）**，3/4 侧面，让他看向画面外（不直视镜头），
保持"他不在意被观察"的气质。背景简化为暗色 tavern 角落，不要喧宾夺主。
